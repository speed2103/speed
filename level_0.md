# Level 0 - The Lobby

## Description
Level 0, known as "The Lobby," is the first and most commonly accessed level of the Backrooms. It is where most entries begin their descent into the Backrooms. However, this level is deceptively chaotic—doors lead to random, unpredictable levels throughout the Backrooms, and completing quests here is essential to progressing.

## Physical Characteristics
- **Environment**: An endless, non-Euclidean maze of empty office spaces and corridors
- **Appearance**: Resembles a typical office building or hotel hallway, with beige walls, fluorescent lighting, and carpeted floors
- **Dimensions**: Appears to be infinitely large and impossible to navigate out of without outside help
- **Architecture**: Hallways are often monotonous, with uniform doors, windows, and lighting
- **Sounds**: Constant, barely audible hum of fluorescent lights; occasional distant mechanical sounds

## Notable Features
- Doors that lead to random levels throughout the Backrooms (not in order)
- Occasional rooms that may contain:
  - Offices with furniture and clues
  - Break rooms with vending machines
  - Storage closets with strange artifacts
  - Bathrooms with mysterious graffiti
- Lighting is always on, though it flickers occasionally
- The environment never changes; all corridors and rooms look identical
- A distinct smell similar to new carpet and office supplies
- **Portal Doors**: Certain doors shimmer faintly and lead to Level 2, Level 7, Level 15, Level 4, or Level 9

## Quests

### Quest 1: Collect the Three Keys
**Objective**: Find three ancient keys hidden throughout Level 0
- **Locations**:
  - First Key: Behind a loose tile in the bathroom on the east corridor
  - Second Key: Inside a vending machine that requires coins to unlock
  - Third Key: In a locked filing cabinet on the 3rd floor offices
- **Reward**: Unlocks the "Dimensional Passage" door leading to Level 2
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Time Limit**: 2 hours (in-game)

### Quest 2: The Code Puzzle
**Objective**: Decipher the code written on office whiteboards throughout the level
- **Clue Distribution**: Each office contains one digit or symbol
- **Full Code**: `4-7-2-1-9`
- **Action**: Enter the code into a keypad on the security office door
- **Reward**: Access to Level 7 emergency exit
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Time Limit**: 3 hours (in-game)

### Quest 3: Survive the Silence
**Objective**: Remain in the central lobby for 1 hour without hearing the alarm
- **Challenge**: Avoid the motion sensors that trigger the alarm
- **Hazard**: If alarm triggers, security entities hunt you for 30 minutes
- **Reward**: Passage to Level 4 (Safe Zone)
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Time Limit**: 1 hour (in-game)

### Quest 4: Feed the Visitor
**Objective**: Gather supplies and leave them in the designated room
- **Items Needed**:
  - 5 cans of food from vending machines
  - 3 bottles of water
  - 1 first aid kit
- **Drop Location**: Room 314 on the 3rd floor
- **Reward**: Access to Level 15 (Higher Dimensions)
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Time Limit**: 4 hours (in-game)

## Hazards
- **Disorientation**: The repeating nature causes severe disorientation and loss of time perception
- **Isolation**: The vast emptiness and silence can cause psychological distress
- **Entities**: Rare encounters with unknown entities
- **No Escape**: Conventional navigation is impossible; no maps work correctly
- **The Watcher**: A tall entity that emerges if you complete all quests—do not confront it

## Resources
- Water from bathroom faucets (drinkable)
- Non-perishable food from vending machines (limited supply)
- Some electrical outlets (though few devices function reliably)
- Coins scattered throughout (needed for vending machines and keypad)

## Inhabitants
- Rare human wanderers
- The Watcher (appears after all quests completed)
- Occasional security entities
- Most areas are empty and devoid of life

## Portal Doors (Random Exits)
Upon completing quests, random shimmer doors appear leading to:
- **Level 2** - The Habitable Zone (completion of Quest 1)
- **Level 4** - The Safe Zone (completion of Quest 3)
- **Level 7** - The Industrial Complex (completion of Quest 2)
- **Level 9** - The Neon City (completion of Quest 4)
- **Level 15** - Higher Dimensions (completion of Quest 4)

## Progression System
- Each level completed grants you experience
- Completing all quests in a level unlocks access to the **next available level**
- Levels are not sequential—you may encounter Level 23 before Level 5
- Each level has its own unique quests and challenges
- The Backrooms is infinite; new levels are always being discovered

## Notes
- Time moves differently in each level
- Completing quests unlocks permanent passages to certain levels
- Some levels can only be accessed after completing specific quests in other levels
- The order is chaos, but progression is real

# Level 0 - The Lobby

## Description
Level 0, known as "The Lobby," is the first and most commonly accessed level of the Backrooms. It is where most entries begin their descent into the Backrooms.

## Physical Characteristics
- **Environment**: An endless, non-Euclidean maze of empty office spaces and corridors
- **Appearance**: Resembles a typical office building or hotel hallway, with beige walls, fluorescent lighting, and carpeted floors
- **Dimensions**: Appears to be infinitely large and impossible to navigate out of without outside help
- **Architecture**: Hallways are often monotonous, with uniform doors, windows, and lighting
- **Sounds**: Constant, barely audible hum of fluorescent lights; occasional distant mechanical sounds

## Notable Features
- Doors that lead to other hallways or dead ends
- Occasional rooms that may contain:
  - Offices with furniture
  - Break rooms with vending machines
  - Storage closets
  - Bathrooms
- Lighting is always on, though it flickers occasionally
- The environment never changes; all corridors and rooms look identical
- A distinct smell similar to new carpet and office supplies

## Hazards
- **Disorientation**: The repeating nature causes severe disorientation and loss of time perception
- **Isolation**: The vast emptiness and silence can cause psychological distress
- **Entities**: Rare encounters with unknown entities (though most inhabitants avoid confrontation)
- **No Escape**: Conventional navigation is impossible; no maps work correctly

## Resources
- Water from bathroom faucets (drinkable)
- Non-perishable food from vending machines (limited supply)
- Some electrical outlets (though few devices function reliably)

## Inhabitants
- Rare human wanderers
- Occasional entities of unknown origin
- Most notably empty and devoid of life

## Entry Points
- Most commonly accessed by "no-clipping" out of reality
- Sudden teleportation from the Frontrooms
- Falling through non-existent floors or walls

## Exit Information
- Extremely difficult to escape intentionally
- Some wanderers have found inexplicable exits after an indefinite period
- Occasionally, ripping through certain walls can lead to other levels
- Some exits lead deeper into the Backrooms (to subsequent levels)

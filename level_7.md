# Level 7 - The Industrial Complex

## Description
Level 7 is a sprawling industrial facility frozen in time. Massive machines line endless corridors, factories stand dormant, and the air is thick with the smell of oil and metal. This level is dangerous but rich with resources and mysteries.

## Physical Characteristics
- **Environment**: Massive industrial factory complex
- **Appearance**: Steel corridors, massive machinery, steam vents, and warning signs
- **Lighting**: Flickering industrial lights, emergency lighting in dark sections
- **Sounds**: Constant hum of machinery, occasional mechanical creaks, steam hisses
- **Temperature**: Warm and humid, especially near active machinery

## Notable Features
- Operational assembly lines (still running on automated power)
- Massive furnaces and smelting facilities
- Storage warehouses containing industrial materials
- Control rooms with outdated technology
- Network of conveyor belts connecting different sections
- Hidden maintenance tunnels beneath the facility

## Quests

### Quest 1: Restart the Main Generator
**Objective**: Navigate to the central power plant and restore full capacity
- **Steps**: Locate fuel, repair damaged systems, synchronize backup generators
- **Challenges**: Machinery malfunctions, steam explosions, structural instability
- **Reward**: Unlocks access to deeper levels and Level 16
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Time Limit**: 5 hours

### Quest 2: Retrieve the Prototype
**Objective**: Find and retrieve an experimental device from the secure lab
- **Location**: Sealed Lab Section 7-D
- **Security**: Automated defense systems still active
- **Reward**: Access to Level 13 (The Technology Nexus)
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Time Limit**: 4 hours

### Quest 3: Catalog the Materials
**Objective**: Document and organize materials in 5 different warehouse sections
- **Task**: Scan items with portable device, organize by type
- **Discovery**: Some items are from other dimensions
- **Reward**: Access to Level 20 (The Storage Dimension)
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Time Limit**: 3 hours

### Quest 4: Fix the Transportation System
**Objective**: Repair the pneumatic transport tubes to reconnect sections
- **Requirements**: Patch leaks, replace broken tubes, restore pressure
- **Benefit**: Faster travel and access to previously isolated areas
- **Reward**: Access to Level 9 (The Neon City)
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Time Limit**: 4 hours

## Hazards
- **Malfunctioning Machinery**: Unpredictable mechanical failures
- **Steam Explosions**: High-pressure systems can rupture without warning
- **Toxic Fumes**: Some areas contain dangerous chemical vapors
- **The Machines**: Autonomous robots that view wanderers as threats
- **Structural Collapse**: Areas may be unstable; falling debris is common

## Resources
- Metal and industrial materials
- Fuel and lubricants
- Electrical components
- Maintenance tools (well-preserved)
- Canned food and water in break rooms

## Inhabitants
- Autonomous maintenance robots (sometimes hostile)
- Rare human scavengers
- Unknown entities in the deepest sections
- The Foreman (legendary wanderer who controls the machines)

## Portal Doors
Shimmer doors lead to:
- **Level 9** - The Neon City (completion of Quest 4)
- **Level 13** - The Technology Nexus (completion of Quest 2)
- **Level 16** - The Steel Dimension (completion of Quest 1)
- **Level 20** - The Storage Dimension (completion of Quest 3)
- **Level 28** - The Clockwork Realm (hidden passage in maintenance tunnels)

## Notes
- The machines are both a blessing and a curse
- Some devices seem to malfunction when watched
- Wanderers claim to hear whispers from the machines
- The deeper you go, the more alive the machines seem

# Level 2 - The Habitable Zone

## Description
Level 2 is one of the few "safe" levels in the Backrooms. It resembles abandoned suburban neighborhoods with functioning infrastructure. Most wanderers consider this a sanctuary, but it holds its own mysteries and challenges.

## Physical Characteristics
- **Environment**: Peaceful suburban streets with abandoned houses
- **Appearance**: 1950s-style neighborhood, perfectly maintained but completely empty
- **Climate**: Temperate, with artificial day-night cycles every 12 hours
- **Structures**: Single-family homes, a small grocery store, a library, and a town square
- **Sounds**: Gentle wind, distant crickets, occasional birdsong

## Notable Features
- Fully stocked grocery stores with fresh (but dated) food
- Working electricity and running water in all buildings
- A central town square with a large fountain
- A library containing maps to other levels
- Houses with usable beds and furniture
- Street lights that turn on and off automatically

## Quests

### Quest 1: Map Collection
**Objective**: Find and collect all 5 maps hidden in the library
- **Locations**: Various shelves in the library marked with red ribbons
- **Maps Lead To**: Level 5, Level 11, Level 19, Level 23, Level 31
- **Reward**: Access to Level 5 (The Gardens)
- **Difficulty**: Easy
- **Time Limit**: 3 hours

### Quest 2: Restore Power to the Town
**Objective**: Repair the generator in the basement of the town hall
- **Requirements**: Gather 3 power cells from different houses
- **Challenges**: Some houses are locked and require solving puzzles
- **Reward**: Unlocks nighttime exploration bonus and access to Level 11
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Time Limit**: 4 hours

### Quest 3: The Garden Mystery
**Objective**: Investigate the overgrown community garden and restore it
- **Tasks**: Clear vines, plant new seeds, find the golden seed
- **Hazard**: Strange creatures lurk among the plants at night
- **Reward**: Access to Level 19 (The Jungle)
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Time Limit**: 5 hours

## Hazards
- **Temporal Anomalies**: Time passes slower here; what feels like 1 hour is 6 hours outside
- **The Silence**: Complete silence can lead to psychological deterioration
- **Night Creatures**: Unknown entities emerge after dark
- **False Sense of Security**: Wanderers often stay too long and lose track of reality

## Resources
- Abundant food and water
- Medical supplies in pharmacies
- Gasoline and tools
- Books and written information about other levels

## Inhabitants
- Occasionally other wanderers seeking refuge
- Night entities (avoid contact)
- Birds and insects (harmless)

## Portal Doors
Shimmer doors lead to:
- **Level 5** - The Gardens (completion of Quest 1)
- **Level 11** - Industrial Farm (completion of Quest 2)
- **Level 19** - The Jungle (completion of Quest 3)
- **Level 23** - Urban Decay (exploration bonus)
- **Level 31** - The Mirror Dimension (hidden quest reward)

## Notes
- This is one of the safest levels for beginners
- The longer you stay, the harder it is to leave
- Other wanderers may try to convince you to stay permanently
- Some houses contain portals to random levels if you look carefully

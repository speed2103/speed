# Level 4 - The Safe Zone

## Description
Level 4 is a mysterious pocket dimension that acts as a refuge for those fleeing danger. It exists in a state of perpetual twilight and is known to shift its geography based on the needs of its inhabitants.

## Physical Characteristics
- **Environment**: Crystalline caverns filled with bioluminescent flora
- **Appearance**: Cave systems with glowing crystals, floating islands, and waterfalls
- **Lighting**: Perpetual twilight from luminescent crystals and plants
- **Gravity**: Slightly altered; objects are 30% lighter
- **Sounds**: Echoing water, crystalline chimes in the wind

## Notable Features
- Multiple interconnected cavern systems
- Floating crystal platforms suspended by unknown forces
- Underground lakes with perfectly clear water
- Crystal formations that hum at specific frequencies
- The Great Crystal: A massive formation at the center that pulses with power
- Harmonic pools that reflect alternate dimensions

## Quests

### Quest 1: Harmony of the Crystals
**Objective**: Activate all 7 crystal nodes by singing the correct frequency
- **Challenge**: Learn the frequencies from harmonic pools scattered around
- **Reward**: Unlocks the "Crystal Gateway" to Level 6 (The Mirror Dimension)
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Time Limit**: 6 hours

### Quest 2: Collect Luminescent Seeds
**Objective**: Gather 12 seeds from rare crystal flowers
- **Locations**: Scattered throughout all cavern levels
- **Hazard**: Some flowers are guarded by crystalline entities
- **Reward**: Access to Level 12 (The Botanical Laboratory)
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Time Limit**: 4 hours

### Quest 3: Restore the Fountain
**Objective**: Clear the central fountain and restore its water flow
- **Tasks**: Remove debris, repair ancient mechanisms, activate water source
- **Secret**: The fountain leads to Level 8 if activated correctly
- **Reward**: Access to Level 8 (The Flooded Halls)
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Time Limit**: 3 hours

## Hazards
- **Altitude Sickness**: Some areas have low oxygen despite appearing open
- **Crystalline Entities**: Defensive creatures made of living crystal
- **Harmonic Resonance**: Certain frequencies can cause disorientation
- **The Gravity Shifts**: Some areas have inverted or sideways gravity

## Resources
- Crystal shards (can be used as tools or lights)
- Clean water from underground springs
- Crystal fruit that sustains wanderers
- Harmonic recordings that hint at other levels

## Inhabitants
- Other wanderers seeking safety
- Crystalline entities (mostly passive)
- Unknown observers in the depths
- The Crystal Keepers (legendary guardians)

## Portal Doors
Shimmer doors lead to:
- **Level 6** - The Mirror Dimension (completion of Quest 1)
- **Level 8** - The Flooded Halls (completion of Quest 3)
- **Level 12** - The Botanical Laboratory (completion of Quest 2)
- **Level 14** - The Underground City (hidden passage)
- **Level 27** - The Crystal Nexus (rare occurrence)

## Notes
- Time moves normally here, unlike Level 2
- The safety is real, but it comes with a cost
- The more you heal here, the harder it is to leave
- Some wanderers have been here for "months" but it's only been days outside
